# Suivi Ventes

## Short description

Record, analyze and audit your team’s daily sales securely.

## Full description

Suivi Ventes is a restricted business application for authorized staff to manage daily sales.

• Record quantities sold by product and date.
• Add or remove products as your business evolves.
• Review a clear dashboard and product performance.
• Filter sales history by month and year.
• Identify the user who added every sale.
• Review a timestamped audit trail.
• Manage Staff, Administrator and SuperAdmin roles.
• Revoke account access immediately when needed.
• Use the application in French or English.

Access is not public: every account is created and controlled by a SuperAdmin.
